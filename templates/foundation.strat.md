## Mission

Close the strategy-execution gap in the AI era by making strategy a living, executable data type for humans and agents alike.

## Vision

A world where every organization—large or small—executes strategy with the precision and adaptability of the best AI agents, turning intent into impact without friction.

## Unlinked Elements

- **Non-Goals**: We are not building general-purpose project management, CRM, or full ERP systems.
- **Red Lines**: Never compromise on openness; never introduce proprietary lock-in; never prioritize features over core format integrity.
